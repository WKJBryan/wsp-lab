### Fixed

- **Answer checker, third rule (ISSUES #30, #31; both closed).** Two weaknesses of the checker of 2026-11-24 are fixed, in `phase3c/rescore.py` (which line of an unboxed answer is read) and `phase3b/scorer.py` (the comparison with the right answer). *Which line is read (ISSUES #31):* after the thinking, the checker took the last line carrying a phrase like "answer is" even when the model ended on a later line without one, and it read a `\boxed{}` only when its braces were nested one level deep at most. Now a complete box anywhere in the answer wins (any depth; blank and unclosed boxes are skipped); without a box the answer is right if either the marked line ("answer is" and the like) or a concluding last line is right, and a line that stops mid-expression, or is a heading or a list number, is not read. *The comparison was stricter than the standard grader, math-verify (ISSUES #30):* the matcher now also accepts answers that differ from the right answer only in formatting — another fraction command or shorthand (`\dfrac{33}{100}`, `\frac43`), a square root without braces, a degree sign, a unit written after the value, thousands separators, a variable in front (`x = 5`), a choice letter in parentheses (`(C)`), a number in another base (`52_8`), a mixed number, a power (`2^{10}`), tuples and fractions compared part by part, a plain word in other capital letters. It only ever adds matches; the comparison of 2026-11-24 is tried first, unchanged. Measured answer by answer on all 23,718 saved answers before anything was re-scored: **943 answers turn from wrong to right and 21 from right to wrong** — 20 of the 21 were accidental hits of the old line rule on text cut at the length limit or on headings; 1 is a box inside the thinking against a different conclusion. Every re-scored file carries the stamp `phase3c/rescore.py+phase3b/scorer.py#d74fdebd340f (ISSUES #19, #30, #31)`. Callers switched to the new reading: `phase5/rescore_finals.py`, `phase8/rescore_arms.py`, `phase11/rescore.py`, `phase8/run_vllm8.py`, `phase11/eval_gemma.py`, `phase3c/grade_reference.py`, `phase14/eval_pass.py` and `phase14/endpoints.py`, which now refuses to run if the six re-scored files it reads were not all made by the checker in the tree.
